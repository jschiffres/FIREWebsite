How it works

Your FIRE number is annual spending divided by your withdrawal rate, so $50,000 at 4% is $1,250,000. The years estimate compounds your balance at the real return and adds your yearly savings until it reaches that number. Read more in the 4% rule explained and What is FIRE?.
