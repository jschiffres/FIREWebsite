Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{ effective_date }}

This policy explains what information {{ company_name }} ("we", "us") collects when you use FI Planner at fiplanner.app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your username, email address, optional name, and a hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google (we never receive your Google password).
  • Plan information: the financial figures you enter, such as ages, income, expenses, savings, account balances and assumptions, and the projections we calculate from them.
  • Billing information: if you subscribe to Premium, payments are handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and subscription status, but never your full card number.
  • Technical information: standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) and the cookies described below.
  • Messages you send us, such as feedback emails.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, calculate and display your financial independence plans.
  • To create and secure your account, verify your email and reset passwords.
  • To process subscriptions and send service emails (verification, receipts-related notices, subscription changes, payment problems).
  • To maintain, debug and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the service: Stripe (payments), Google (email delivery and optional sign-in), our hosting provider, and content delivery networks (such as jsDelivr) that serve open-source libraries to your browser. These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in (session cookie) and to protect forms (CSRF cookie). Your browser's session and local storage are used for interface preferences, such as the dashboard tab you last viewed, and to keep a draft of an unfinished survey. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete individual plans at any time. To delete your account and associated data, use the Delete account option in My Account (or email us); we will do so promptly, except where we must retain records (for example, payment records) to meet legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can export plan data from the dashboard (Premium) and manage your subscription from My Account. For any request, contact {{ contact_email }}.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections, hashed passwords and access controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

FI Planner is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If changes are material we will notify you by email or within the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions? Email {{ contact_email }}.
