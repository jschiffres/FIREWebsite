Assumptions & Disclaimer

FI Planner is an educational projection tool. It is not financial, tax, or investment advice. Projections are estimates based on the numbers you enter; actual results will differ.

How projections work

  • Balances grow once per year at the rate of return you enter for each account.
  • Each year, income minus expenses is saved and allocated to your accounts according to your savings settings.
  • Employer matches are added on top of your own contributions.
  • Years where expenses exceed income are drawn down from your net worth and flagged as shortfalls.
  • The Magic Number is your annual recurring expenses divided by the safe withdrawal rate (a 4% rule gives 25× expenses).
  • FI age is the first year your net worth meets or exceeds the Magic Number.

What is not modeled

  • Market volatility and sequence-of-returns risk; returns are constant.
  • Taxes on withdrawals and changes to tax law or contribution limits.
  • Social Security, pensions, or healthcare cost changes in retirement.
