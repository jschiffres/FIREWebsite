How it works

Your Coast FI number is your full FI number (spending ÷ withdrawal rate) divided by (1 + real return) raised to the years until retirement. If you already have at least that much invested, you can stop saving for retirement and only cover current living costs. Read What is Coast FIRE? for a worked example, or use the FIRE calculator for your full FI number.
